# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the AgriSmart AI community respectful and
welcoming for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or
personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming, clear, and constructive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus discussion on ideas, evidence, and the project’s goals.
- Respect privacy and never share another person’s personal information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Threats, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Misrepresenting project results, model performance, or another contributor’s work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow
this code. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent
exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer or institution
placeholder. Include only the information needed to assess the issue. Reports will
be handled respectfully and as confidentially as practical.
